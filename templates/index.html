An Expert Advisor (EA) is a computer program initially designed to assist professional Forex traders in identifying profitable trades. Over time, it has evolved into a fully automated trading algorithm that operates independently. An automated EA follows strict, predefined rules based on a specific trading strategy, executing trades without any emotional interference. If the strategy has an edge in the market, the EA should, in theory, generate profits.

Other EAs uses mathematical strategy that is fitted to a specific currency pair, given a specific timeframe. This works well for backtesting, but if the EA is over fitted to the backtesting data, it won't work in different situations and is vunerable to market anomalies. This the EA has high risk although not seeming so.

What sets Macro Perspective EAs apart is that, unlike most others, uses real time market analysis. It checks the news, uses fundamental analysis, does not trade when volatility is high, and integrates market sentiment into its trading decisions. Thus, our EA cannot fully be backtested, but will withstand any market anomilies, and is not overfitted to a specifix scenario. fit for any currency pair, any condition.

How the EA Works:

- Continuous Market Evaluation: The program continuously monitors the market based on predefined parameters, automatically deciding when to trade or hold off.

- Timed Trade Execution: It may open trades on specified intervals, depending on market conditions, focusing on specific currency pairs during their active hours.

- Volatility Management: The EA automatically pauses trading when the market becomes volatile or other specified conditions aren't met, ensuring trades are only executed under favorable circumstances.

- Controlled Trade Closure: Trades are closed either when they reach a profitable level or after a specified duration, with a limited number of trades open at any given time to minimize risk.

- Trailing Take-Profit: The program uses an automatic trailing take-profit feature, allowing profits to be maximized while maintaining control over risk.

- Linear Risk Management: Risk is managed in a linear fashion, up to a defined point, ensuring consistent exposure.

- Dynamic Trading Plan: The EA adapts dynamically to changing market conditions, volatility, sentiment, and news, adjusting its strategy accordingly.

- Customizable Settings:** The current settings make this an automated, low-risk, conservative program that trades only under normal market conditions. However, by adjusting a few parameters, I can modify the risk level and trading conditions as needed. The program can even accept manual input on the fly.
